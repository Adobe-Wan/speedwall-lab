# QA pass, 2026-10-07 (alpha v0.5 → v0.5.1)

Four QA passes were made on v0.5: controls and input; simulation, views and HUD; accessibility and responsive layout; and a code review. Every finding was reproduced in headless Chromium (Playwright) before it was fixed. Real controllers, a real screen reader and iOS were not tested.

## Fixed in v0.5.1

**Controls**
- **Wizard capture.**
  - A held key, or releasing a just-bound axis, no longer binds the next step. The wizard waits for the controls to settle.
  - Enter and Space on its buttons press the buttons.
  - Back undoes the step, including the step an axis covered.
  - Skipped steps keep the keyboard default.
  - Focus moves to each step, and step changes are announced.
  - A controller connected mid-step works.
- **Inputs.** Hats that report as axes (resting at about 1.29) are ignored. A throttle lever at idle reads 0 % instead of handing control back to the on-screen throttle. A roll axis at full deflection stays analog.
- **Import.**
  - Starts from the keyboard defaults and replaces only the actions the file binds.
  - Identical sticks are numbered by their order in the file.
  - Modifier combos and hats are skipped, and the result message says so.
  - Names such as `__proto__` and `constructor` are rejected.
  - The result message stays visible.
- **Saved settings** are validated on load, so a damaged one can't break the page.
- **Dialog.** The first-run wizard doesn't reopen after you close it. There is one refresh loop instead of one per open. The summary shows keyboard input, and the ship doesn't fly while the dialog is open.

**Page**
- **Space and Enter** go to a focused button only when you reached it with Tab. A clicked button no longer catches Space, which is strafe up.
- **Taking over a maneuver** with keys or a controller ends it and clears the throttle, pad and boost lock it had set.
- **Ctrl+W guard.** The leave-page prompt now stays on unless the keyboard lock actually succeeded.
- **Keyboard access.** Hold-to-boost and the Roll buttons work with the keyboard. The throttle's snap to zero applies to dragging only, so the arrow keys work. Single-key shortcuts don't fire inside form controls.
- **"Dodge room"** now shows the room left, which is the cross-section minus your current sideways speed. Captions use the same number.
- **Captions and copy.**
  - Captions now cover flying backwards and bleeding speed after boost is released.
  - The "Top forward speed" caption appears only at the nose.
  - The maneuver copy matches the simulation (ease off, shoot-back corkscrew, over-spinning).
- **Spacebrake** tapers off near zero instead of overshooting into reverse.
- **Unlimited boost** shows a full tank and clears the lockout.
- **Reversing:** the edge arrow points down when the TVI is straight behind, and the HUD speed is signed.
- **Top-down view** includes vertical speed.
- **Trail and timing.** The trail lasts about 3 s of sim time at any frame rate. The page keeps sim time on slow machines (frames up to 0.25 s).
- **No WebGL:** the page runs on the top-down view and says why.
- **Screen readers:** the live region announces changes (boost, at the limit, lockout) instead of the speed every 2 s.
- **Layout.**
  - Camera buttons have a visible focus ring and an on-state that isn't colour alone.
  - Control borders and the strafe pad edge have 3:1 contrast, and the roll-rate slider has its own row.
  - Binding rows stack on phones, view titles are hidden when tabs show, and the smallest HUD text is larger.
  - Controls is the main header button.

## Proposed UI updates (1–6 and 8 shipped in v0.6; 7 and 9 open)
1. **Phones:** keep the view on screen while flying, with a sticky view or a bottom control dock (boost, throttle, strafe), and a side-by-side landscape layout.
2. **Maneuvers:**
   - Make them the main lessons, directly under the views.
   - While one runs, show a "running / press any control to take over" banner.
   - When it ends, show a result chip (simulator vs in game).
3. **Dodge room:**
   - Show it as a two-sided bar (left / right).
   - Keep the HUD clear of the TVI in the 8–20° band the corkscrews use. (Replaced in v0.7: the HUD has a fixed, symmetric layout and the TVI is drawn above it.)
   - Show the roll rate that would anchor the TVI.
4. **Controls flow:**
   - Show what each step captured, with Undo.
   - Rebind one row at a time, and warn about conflicts.
   - Let the device checkboxes shape the prompts, and show the automatic gamepad mapping.
5. **Key hints on buttons**, e.g. Boost [L Shift], Roll [Q]/[E]. Add a setting to turn off single-key shortcuts.
6. **Performance (PLAN §9):** render on demand and pause off-screen views. Draw calls are 16–18 per frame against a budget of 12.
7. **Screen readers:** expose the readouts, and give each view a text summary.
8. **Wide screens (2560 px):** use the extra width.
9. **Labels:** add halos to the egg labels, and stop them piling up at the tail when reversing.
