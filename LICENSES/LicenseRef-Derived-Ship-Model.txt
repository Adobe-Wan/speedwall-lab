Derived ship model (not an open licence)

The files under site/assets/ships/ are derived from third-party meshes for
this non-profit educational fan project (see NOTICE.md "Ship model" and
docs/adr/004-ship-models.md). Each file's source and licence label is recorded
in NOTICE.md; for the Gladius it is the Sketchfab model by lechatfantome,
labelled CC BY 4.0, likely a game extraction, provenance unverified.

These files are excluded from the MIT and CC BY 4.0 grants of this
repository. They are not offered for reuse, and they are removed on request.
