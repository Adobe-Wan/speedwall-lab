How to read it. Your ship sits at your current velocity: the farther from the centre cross (stopped), the faster you're moving, in that direction. Amber egg: top speed in each direction while boosting (520 forward, 394 sideways, 268 back). Blue ball: top speed without boost (SCM, 225 every way). White ring: how much more sideways speed you can add at your forward speed; it turns red at the limit. TVI (the circle with wings in the cockpit): where you're really going. Decoupled: the ship keeps drifting when you let go; thrusters only push.

Physics: the tested core model (packages/core), fitted to the author's in-game measurements. All 30 measured settle speeds match within 1%; the wall and boost-release traces mostly match, and the misses are listed in docs/physics-fit.md. Decoupled mode only. Roll is a preview: each corkscrew lists its in-game result to compare.

Measurements: AdobeWan, in-game tests (Oct 2026). Original directional-G research: SC Ships Performances Viewer by Olakeen, used with permission.
