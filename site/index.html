How to read it: the amber egg is every velocity the Gladius can reach while boosting; the blue sphere is SCM (225 m/s). The ship sits on your velocity: the further it is from the grey rest cross, the faster you are going, and in that direction. The white ring is the egg's cross-section at your forward speed; the white lines from the ship to the ring are how much more you can strafe each way before you hit the wall.

Physics: the tested core model (packages/core), fitted to the author's in-game measurements. All 30 measured settle speeds match within 1%; the wall and boost-release traces mostly match, and the misses are listed in docs/physics-fit.md. Decoupled mode only. Roll is a preview: each corkscrew lists its in-game result to compare.

Measurements: AdobeWan, in-game tests (Oct 2026). Original directional-G research: SC Ships Performances Viewer by Olakeen, used with permission.
